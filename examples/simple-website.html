Welcome hihisf My Website

This is an example of a simple website with the MakeWebsiteAIReady chatbot embedded.

How it works:
  1. Register at MakeWebsiteAIReady dashboard
  2. Add your website and verify it
  3. Upload your documents (PDFs, DOCX, etc.)
  4. Copy the embed code and paste it in your website
  5. The chatbot icon appears - your visitors can now ask questions!

The chatbot below will only answer questions from your uploaded documents. It has strong guardrails to prevent off-topic conversations.

Controls

You can control the widget prasdfgogrammatically:

  • MakeWebsiteAIReady.open() - Open the chat
  • MakeWebsiteAIReady.close() - Close the chat
  • MakeWebsiteAIReady.hide() - Hide completely
  • MakeWebsiteAIReady.show() - Show again
  • MakeWebsiteAIReady.setOpacity(0.8) - Set transparency
  • MakeWebsiteAIReady.resize('large') - Resize (small/medium/large)
